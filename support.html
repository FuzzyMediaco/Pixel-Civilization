Game support

Find a saved island

Open More → Saves on the device where you built the island. For cloud saves, sign in with the email address you used to save it. Offline play keeps your progress on that device.

Restore a purchase

Sign in to your original game account, open the store in Pixel Civilization and choose Restore purchases while connected. Use the same App Store or Google Play account that paid for the purchase.

Manage a subscription

Open your store’s subscription manager to change plans or cancel renewal. For a web subscription, use the billing link in the game store. A one-time era or mode purchase stays on your game account.

Report a game problem

Include your device model, the game mode and what you did just before the problem. The issue tracker is public. Leave out your email, purchase token, password and payment details.
