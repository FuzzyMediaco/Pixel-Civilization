## Architecture and safety contracts

- Original geometry lives in `src/game/designs/`. Build menu and Atlas use canonical IDs, variants and game footprints. Era art has lazy loaders in `src/game/designs/eraArt/` and eager labels in `src/game/eraNames/`. Keep the Forum pilot isolated when overriding Rome Aqueduct City. The Handbook, Atlas, Build and offline paths must all resolve the same selection.
- The three civic additions are `climbing_city_hall`, `civic_viaduct_exchange` and `levitating_civic_tower`. Only new entries received new stats. The viaduct was the additional administration building chosen for the open-ended brief. The tallest levitating towers reach 36.25–37.5 in-game metres.
- The two new defense IDs are `drone_swarm_base` and `underground_missile_silo`. The clock and animated visual poses live in `src/game/defenseDisplay.ts`; geometry lives in the matching files under `src/game/designs/`. Keep per-instance motion independent of cached base geometry. Eighty berths were checked across 20 swarm bases. [Matched live phone stills and animations](../docs/review/military-exercise/README.md).
- Joined bridges live in `bridgeWorks.ts`, `bridgeBay.ts`, `bridgeLandmarks.ts` and `engineeringView.ts` under `src/game/`. Keep separate collision/picking decks and moving trim, supports and counterweights. Check one, three, six and twelve interior pieces, old width data, both axes, raised and lowered lift, highway and rail. The final gallery is authoritative for curb trim. The Marina Cut city screenshots were taken just before that last trim edit.
- Never change existing IDs, footprints, costs, saves, economic rules, simulation behavior, products, prices, consent, terms, legal links, purchase restoration or store listings for an art change. Use original characters. Building art should remain legible at street, overhead and phone scales. Rework rectangular or look-alike buildings even when a geometry threshold passes.
- Before merging, run `npm run lint`, `npm test` and `npm run build`, then require the named **Game checks** workflow to succeed. Start the gallery with `bash scripts/gallery-server.sh`. Example capture command is `node scripts/shoot-gallery.mjs 'cat=all&ids=public_library&era=ancient-china&mode=full&cell=180' /tmp/library.webp`. Use an isolated base checkout for the before image. Use `ATLAS_IDS=<ids> node --import tsx scripts/render-handbook.mjs` for changed Original sheets. Recompute silhouettes and mesh budgets and append a dated line to [PROGRESS](../docs/redesign/PROGRESS.md) after each merge.
