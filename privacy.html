Pixel Civilization

Privacy

Pixel Civilization can keep your islands on your device. Signing in adds online saves, purchases and access to Pix. This page explains what each feature needs.

Your account and islands

The game stores your email address, a password hash, your account ID and sign-in sessions. An online save contains your island layout, settings and simulation progress. The game uses this information to sign you in, restore purchases and synchronize your islands. Local islands are stored in your browser or app.

Purchases

When you buy something, the App Store, Google Play or Stripe processes the payment. Pixel Civilization checks the transaction with the provider and links the product, transaction identifier and entitlement status to your game account. The game receives no full card numbers. Payment providers keep their own billing records.

Pix

When you choose an AI provider and send a request, Pix sends your message, relevant conversation context and game information to that provider to generate the response. The game service records request counts, model and token usage to enforce your plan. It does not store the text of your prompt or the generated response on its server. Each selected provider applies its API data policies. Avoid sharing sensitive information with Pix.

Hosting and protection

Cloudflare hosts the game and online saves. The hosted website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits and page performance. The service uses temporary request and sign-in counters to limit abuse. Store editions do not include an advertising SDK. External websites and payment providers apply their own privacy practices.

Your choices

You can play the downloadable game offline, use local islands and leave Pix unused. Use the account deletion page to remove your game account and cloud saves. To remove local islands too, clear the app’s data or your browser’s site storage. You can manage an Apple or Google subscription in that store.
