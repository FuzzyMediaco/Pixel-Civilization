Can I keep playing the same island?

Open More → Account on the device or website where your island already lives. Create an account or sign in, sync your saves, then sign in on your other device. You can also keep playing as a guest.

How do updates work?

Open the installed game while online to check for updates. The next launch uses the new edition once the download finishes. You can keep building offline while you wait.
