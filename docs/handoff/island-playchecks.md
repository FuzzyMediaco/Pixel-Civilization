# Check the three example islands

Open **Game menu → Example islands**. Each world has an independent God-mode save and seven guided stops. Open **Island guide** at the bottom of the map to jump to a stop. **Example islands → Return to my island** takes you back to your own city. The handbook’s **Worlds** chapter opens each world directly. Under each island, choose **See all viewpoints** for photographs of every stop.

| Island | First experiment |
| --- | --- |
| **Pelican Reach** | Follow the passenger ferry through the lift at Marina Cut. Send a naval vessel beyond the estuary. Send a smaller launch through the western canal to the raised pond and compare the width and headroom it needs. |
| **Copper Gorge** | Follow the mountain supply across a bend and bridge to the reservoir and five farm fields. Open the millrace dam and inspect the change in hydroelectric head. Compare the reclaimed desert cottage, the mine terraces and the winding gorge. |
| **Switchyard City** | Ride the train through the night market, inspect a home’s driveway or a shop’s banking frontage, then compare the municipal fire crew, flying taxis and Spaceport. Select a place in the guide to travel directly there. |

## Requests checked in play

| Request | How to verify it |
| --- | --- |
| Shape the island and ocean | Open **Land & water**. Use a brush on the map or enter tile coordinates. Preview the cost and affected sites, apply the change, then undo it. Try a bay, peninsula, hill, mountain, canyon, river, pond and sand side by side. The landform elements also change the ground under neighboring sites. Older saves preserve the original shoreline. |
| Connect aqueducts | Start beside a mountain spring or working pump. Trace a bend, add a branch and change support height. Inspect the available head and flow. End beside a pond, farm or reservoir, or leave a side outlet open. An upper branch can run dry even when the intake is supplied. |
| Cross waterways and canyons | Draw between two dry banks. Preview arches, trusses, suspension spans and lifts, then compare the deck type, ramps and headroom. A low deck can cut a ferry route. An automatic lift stops vehicles while a ship passes. |
| Build a water city | Dredge a continuous channel into the sea. The harbor master uses beam, draft, overhead clearance, bend geometry and gates to plan a voyage. Ferries and water taxis carry residents between walkable landings. Barges deliver freight. Canals can irrigate land and give bank homes a local amenity. |
| Use fresh water for hydropower | A reservoir stores delivered water. A hydro plant needs a river or canal and usable head, including on a previously unedited map. A salt bay or ocean site fails the placement check. Opening a dam changes its water levels and output. |
| Build road, rail and water transit | Give each line a depot or landing, suitable connected stops, and a continuous round trip. Watch departures, boarding, transfers, returns and deliveries. Completed passenger journeys relieve traffic. The transit report shows demand, coverage and waits. Bus stops remain one tile and finish upgrading at level three. |
| Run cars between buildings | Join a house driveway to a shop entrance with a continuous local street. Inspect a road tile in **Connections** to find a journey. In **Layers → Traffic**, compare the route at rush hour and late at night. A detached street or a spur with no destination stays empty. |
| Control roads and traffic | Compare local roads, highways and freeways, set a lane count within the road class, and draw an interchange. Try diamond, trumpet or directional layouts. Signals, lane direction, queueing and bridge closures affect vehicle movement. |
| Grow wealth and build projects | Open **City wealth** to inspect the treasury, households, businesses and transfers. Staffed jobs earn income, residents spend, and crime and confidence affect trade. Peak wealth opens 22 projects in Real and Survival. God mode can use every project and still observe its accounts. |
| Watch projects operate | Follow the airport flight and offshore patrol in Pelican Reach, the working open pit and portal in Copper Gorge, or a rocket countdown and flying taxi in Switchyard City. Site controls show staffing, utilities, readiness and the result of a completed journey. |
| Join neighboring buildings | In **Connections**, inspect joined townhomes, a house driveway, a backyard garden, a bank or ATM beside retail, five crop plots around a farmhouse, and a church beside a graveyard. Stations face their service network. The design atlas links each of the 204 elements to an example or a relevant lesson. |
| Upgrade in place | Select a building and inspect its next-level conditions, price and footprint. Production, occupancy, customers, visitors or departures provide the progress appropriate to its use. Keep neighboring space free for expansions. God mode changes level and style directly. The top-level Cheesesteak Spot is a walk-in sandwich. |
| Feed households and supply utilities | Farms and gardens draw water, produce food and support local happiness. Farm gates distribute part of their harvest directly. Grocers sell district stock and can buy affordable imports. Additional residents increase water and power demand. Sustained hunger, dry taps or blackouts drive emigration after a settling period. |
| Operate civic services | Garbage, schools, parks, employment, drinking water and power all enter the city's needs. A staffed government office enables professional fire, police and defense services and carries a small happiness cost. Agriculture and recreation ease crime pressure. |
| Respond to fire and night conditions | Ordinary fire calls begin after the Stage 4 settling period in Real and Survival, when the fire department is available. The safety panel compares crews with population and station coverage. Engines and fireboats need a route and water. Night changes lighting, solar supply, street traffic, depot intervals and crime. |
| Control the simulation | God controls provide time steps, weather, city and local overrides, routes, projects, incidents and snapshots. Real and Survival unlock land tools in stages. Survival adds maintenance, food and treasury pressure, fires and late defense incidents. |
| Use the menus | Build, Layers, View and Status have labeled shortcuts. The same locally bundled DM Sans is used by the game, handbook and field guide. Panels share controls for closing, keyboard focus and mobile layouts. |

The illustrated handbook includes photographs of the running worlds, the engineering tool gallery and all 204 design sheets. The scenario, engineering, transit, city economy and construction tests exercise the same code as the game. Browser checks cover desktop and phone-sized layouts, all tool kinds, drawing a bend, applying and undoing work, and saving and reopening example cities.
