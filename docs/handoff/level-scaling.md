# Level scaling (level_scaling agent)

Owner ask: *every benefit a civilization element offers must scale with its level, game wide.*
The input audit is `docs/review/level-scaling-audit.md` (review_systems); row numbers (#) below refer to it.

## Model

There is one module for this, `src/game/levelScaling.ts`. Every system multiplies an element's Lv.1 figure by
`levelMultiplier(curve, level)`. Levels are clamped to 1..5, so a road at Lv.11 never gets more than Lv.5 output.

| curve | formula | Lv1→Lv5 | used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `output` | 2^(L-1) | 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 | population, jobs, power, water, waste, education, recreation, government, police, military, export/visitor income, preschool seats, pond irrigation, pumped-storage capacity & pump rate, district-heat savings, floodplain rain waste, attendance, waterfront amenity |
| `crop` | 2.35^(L-1) | …30.5 | farm, field and farm-module harvest; `farm_houses` (kept: fields sprawl as they level) |
| `retail` | 2.25^(L-1) | …25.6 | grocer, restaurant, bakery, exchange and mall food throughput (kept) |
| `draw` | max(1, round(0.75·2^(L-1))) | 1, 2, 3, 6, 12 | power and water drawn by consumers (kept) |
| `trips` | 1.7^(L-1) | …8.35 | road trips generated, transit riders generated by each site |
| `reach` | 2^(0.75(L-1)) | …8 | faith community strength, defender rosters (soldiers, tanks, cops…) |
| `storage` | 2^(0.75(L-1)) | …8 | pond cistern, rainwater-cathedral cistern, rail freight stockpiles |
| `radius` | 1 + 0.25(L-1) | …2.0 | police and fire coverage, nuisance radius, heatmap provider radii, transit-stop draw |
| `amenity` | 2^((L-1)/2) | …4 | flat parcel-edge bonuses (cloister/ceremonial recreation, service waste, ceremonial education, heat exchange, garden food) |
| `pollution` | 2^((L-1)/2) | …4 | pollution emitted, and pollution absorbed by trees and parks |
| `desirability` | 1 + 0.25(L-1) | …2.0 | land-value strength (radius grows by +0.75 tiles per level) |
| `upkeep` | 2^(0.6(L-1)) | …5.28 | running cost `cost × 0.008 × upkeep(L)`. Roads keep `0.04 × L` |

`cityDynamics.elementLevelStats(def, level)` returns what an element supplies at a given level before situational factors
(population, jobs, power/powerDraw, water/waterDraw, waste, food, education, recreation, government, police, military,
income, policeRadius, fireRadius, nuisanceRadius, pollution, upkeep, trips). The aggregation loop in `simulation.ts`
now reads these figures directly and multiplies them by synergy, disrepair, weather and so on, so this one function is
both the production formula and the source for UI numbers. `BottomDock.formatElementStatSummary(def, level = 1)` now
calls it. Only the logic function changed, not the JSX. See the follow-ups below.

## What changed (all intentional)

- **Coverage radii** (#16, #17): police and fire radius went from ×(1+0.18(L-1)) (Lv5 ×1.72) to `radius` (Lv5 ×2.0,
  so 4× the area). Heatmap radii use the same helper (#59). The nuisance radius now scales (#21).
- **Pollution** (#20): went from ×(1+0.3(L-1)) (Lv5 2.2×) to 4× at Lv5. A Lv.5 coal plant gives 16× the power for 4×
  the smog. Absorbers clean 4× as much.
- **Upkeep** (#32): went from L^0.8 (Lv5 3.6×) to 5.28× at Lv5.
- **Desirability** (#23): strength goes up to 2.0× (was 1.8×) and the radius gains +0.75 tiles per level (was +0.5).
- **Parcel-edge bonuses** (#25) were flat and now scale with `amenity`.
- **Waterfront home recreation** (#26): was `min(8, 2L)`, now `2 × output(L)`.
- **Irrigation ponds** (#37): outflow and rain inflow use `output` (was linear). The cistern uses `storage`, exposed as
  `cityEconomy.pondCisternCapacity(level)`. Water demand from dry growers is now Σ 0.18·output(L) of each grower
  (it used to ignore their level).
- **Rainwater cathedral cistern** (#38): `180 × storage(L)`.
- **Pumped storage** (#41): capacity is `65 × output(L)` (was 65L), and the charge rate cap is `4 × output(L)` (was a
  flat 4).
- **District heat** (#39) and **floodplain rain waste** (#40) went from linear to `output`.
- **Attendance** (#42) went from linear to `output`, the same curve as education and recreation supply.
- **Faith strength** (#52) went from 1+0.45(L-1) to `reach` (Lv5 8×). Beds keep their table [8, 15, 25, 40, 60].
- **Defender rosters** (#19) went from 1+0.55(L-1) to `reach`. The renderer's spawn caps are unchanged.
- **Transit** (#49): each catchment site's riders follow `trips` (was √L), consistent with the road trips that the same
  site generates. A stop's own level multiplies its draw by `radius`. The coverage weight follows `output` (was L).
- **Rail freight stockpile and accrual** went from L to `storage`.
- **Dead stats wired** (#30, #31):
  - `income` was ignored for 46 elements. Every non-housing site with income that is not already an exporter now
    earns visitor spending of `income × output(L) × 0.12` (half the export rate), scaled by staffing, traffic and land
    value like exports.
  - Grocers and restaurants are excluded because they already earn through food sales.
  - `food` was ignored on non-crop, non-retail sites. Docks, boats, windmills, monasteries and machu_picchu now
    produce a harvest of `food × output(L) × 0.10`.
  - Malls are now a retail food site (food court) and also import groceries.
- `transitRelief()` was dead code and has been removed (balance round 2). Live relief comes from `mobility.reliefAtStops`.

Unchanged on purpose: the crop, retail, draw and trip curves; road capacity (it has its own lane model, levels 1..11);
sanctuary beds; blessing durations; the upgrade activity goal (×L time gate); vessel hull growth; the rail car table.

## Tests

`tests/levelScaling.test.ts` (5 tests):
- Every curve starts at 1 and rises strictly. Radii are at most 2× at Lv5. Levels outside 1..5 are clamped.
- For **every catalog element** that is not a network, each field of `elementLevelStats` stays the same or rises from
  Lv1 to Lv5. The element's primary metric (the first non-zero benefit) rises strictly, and every benefit it
  advertises is higher at Lv5 than at Lv1.
- The owner's examples: farms, landfill, hydro dam, nuclear plant, mall, and nuclear upkeep.
- In the live simulation (a road town in god mode), water, waste, education, recreation and power metrics rise
  strictly with the level of wells, landfill, elementary_school, little_parks and coal_powerplants.
- In the live economy, farm yield, grocer retail, mall retail, mall visitor income and bank income rise strictly with
  level.

No existing expectation needed changing. With only my files on top of HEAD: `npx tsc --noEmit` is clean and
`npm test` gives 343/343. The full working tree shows failures in design-style and handbook tests. Those come from
other agents' design work in progress, not from these files.

## Follow-ups for other owners (not edited)

- `src/components/ExpansionDetails.tsx:70` shows the pond cistern as `el.level * 36`. Use
  `pondCisternCapacity(el.level)` from cityEconomy.
- `src/components/BottomDock.tsx`, at the palette card call `formatElementStatSummary(def)`: pass
  `brush.defId === def.id ? brush.level : 1` so god-mode brush levels show their scaled output.
- These are outside this agent's files and still don't scale with level:
  - `terrain.ts` hydraulic channel output `12 × L` (#35) and reservoir storage `100 × L` (#36).
  - `projectOperations.ts` fire crews `min(L, staff)` (#18) and aviation seats (#51).
- Design or balance questions for the owner:
  - Passive nature gets full `output` scaling with no upkeep (#33).
  - Output per tile on 1×1 sites (wind turbines, wells) at Lv5 (#56).
  - The growth step's housing base ignores land value and synergies (#2).
  - The nuisance happiness penalty counts homes rather than residents (#22).
