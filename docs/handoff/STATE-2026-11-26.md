## What the game is now
A voxel island city builder. It is live at https://pixelcivilization.app, with Android, Windows and iOS editions (portable 1.2.1).
- **294 elements** across 13 categories:

  | Category | Elements |
  |---|---|
  | Police/military | 44 |
  | Commerce | 36 |
  | Agriculture | 26 |
  | Civic/parks | 25 |
  | Roads/transit | 24 |
  | Wealth projects | 23 |
  | Nature | 22 |
  | Housing | 18 |
  | Power/waste | 18 |
  | Landmarks | 18 |
  | Maritime | 16 |
  | Water | 12 |
  | Faith | 12 |

  Each has up to 5 levels and 4 visual variants.
- **Ten playable eras** (Ancient Egypt, Rome with two art directions, China, Greece, Medieval Europe, Renaissance Italy, 3027…). Each re-skins the same city, and there are 30 sample tour islands.
- **Modes:** Real, Idle and Manager are free. Survival and God are paid. Islands are saved through accounts plus offline/portable saves.
- **Store and Pix:**
  - era packs at $0.99, Survival at $1.99, God at $3.99;
  - "Pix" AI-advisor monthly plans from $4.99 to $99.99, through Stripe and the native stores.

  See `docs/store-billing.md` and `docs/time-periods.md`.
- **Learning material:** the illustrated Handbook, the Atlas/field guide, and example islands. Round 2 added Meridian Fair, College Walk, Tomorrow Quay and Four-herd valley.

## What Claude built (all merged into `main`)

### Round 1: framework, systems and the original 149-element redesign
- **Design system:**
  - per-element design registry, with level names, growth notes and footprints up to 4×4;
  - the voxel `kit.ts`;
  - the element gallery tool (`gallery.html`, `scripts/shoot-gallery.mjs`).
- **The original 149 elements redesigned** at every level and variant, by 8 parallel design agents.
- **Living-city systems:**
  - traffic with rush hours;
  - pollution and land-value fields;
  - neighbour synergies;
  - live solar and wind power;
  - upkeep;
  - roads that become bridges over water;
  - boats that need docks;
  - cars kept off footpaths;
  - smoke, beacons and rotors;
  - car-following in traffic;
  - land value, pollution and traffic heatmaps;
  - history sparklines and trophies.

### Round 2: review, rebalance and redesign (`docs/review/ROUND2-REPORT.md`)
- **Reviews:**
  - `systems-review.md`
  - `level-scaling-audit.md`
  - `ui-review.md` (with mockups)
  - `trains-review.md`
  - `new-elements-proposal.md` (25 element ideas)
- **Level scaling:** `levelScaling.ts`. Every benefit, cost, radius and pollution value scales with level, and the UI shows the same numbers the simulation uses.
- **Balance:**
  - fixed money loop: trade and sales tax, household spending, tourism income;
  - a visible population limiter;
  - day-averaged happiness;
  - rebalanced needs, defense, crime and traffic;
  - stages 13–20 made reachable;
  - tax rate and stage grants.
- **Trains:**
  - short block reservations, lines with several trains, demand-based passengers;
  - track level sets the speed limit, and trains accelerate and brake;
  - world-scale rolling stock in 5 eras;
  - a phone-friendly Rail Operations panel with a tap-stations line editor.

  Freight waiting at red signals fell from 44% to 2%, and mean passenger wait from 410 s to 38 s.
- **Phone-first UI:**
  - status chips, an alert pill, and Build/Layers/City/Transit/More tabs;
  - one bottom-sheet component;
  - a labelled inspector;
  - Undo toasts;
  - City Issues with Fix buttons.
- **Redesigns:**
  - 11 schools;
  - 20 farm elements;
  - 22 wealth projects;
  - ferry landings and taxi ranks.
- **Animated livestock** that walk, graze and peck in their pens.
- **8 historical wonders:** Colosseum, Stonehenge, Parthenon, Great Wall, Chichen Itza, Moai, Angkor Wat and Machu Picchu.
- A ChatGPT session then integrated all of this into the preview, handbook and field guide (`29af172`, `docs/handoff/preview-round2-playcheck.md`).

Everything since then — eras, accounts, store, native apps, Idle/Manager modes, bulk tools, 294 elements — came from other sessions, not Claude.

## How to work on it
- **Checks:** `npm run lint`, `npm test`, `npm run build`. Preview bundle: `node scripts/build-preview.mjs ./portable`.
- **Gallery:**
  - start the server with `./scripts/gallery-server.sh`;
  - render with `node scripts/shoot-gallery.mjs "cat=<file>&ids=a,b&mode=full" out.png`;
  - game shots with `scripts/shoot-game.mjs` (inject saves with `page.addInitScript`).
- **Docs:** design rules are in `docs/handoff/DESIGN_BRIEF.md`; per-area notes are in `docs/handoff/*.md`.
