# 25 Proposed New Elements

Each proposal fills a concrete gap in the current 204-element roster and its systems. They are grouped by the problem they solve.

Format per element: **stage · footprint Lv1→Lv5 · primary effect** · the gap it fills · how it looks and levels up (V0–V3 are the four visual styles).

---

## A. Health & wellbeing. The game has no healthcare at all

Today happiness is a list of utility checks. There is no "people get sick / get old" pressure, and no building that heals.
Proposal: add a **Health** need (demand ≈ 1.1 × citizen units, rising with pollution and density). It is served by these three. Low health raises emigration and lowers growth. Pandemics are a rare event, on the same scale as fires.

1. **Neighborhood Clinic** — S2 · 1×1→2×2 · +Health (radius 6→11), small jobs.
   *Gap:* early-game health service and a reason to space homes around a hub.
   *Look:* white cottage with a red-cross sign and an ambulance bay. Levels add a pharmacy wing, a garden courtyard, then a rooftop helipad. V0 country doctor / V1 modern glass / V2 adobe / V3 Scandinavian timber.

2. **General Hospital** — S7 · 2×2→4×3 · +Health (big radius), +jobs, ambulance dispatch (cuts fire and disaster casualties).
   *Gap:* mid/late-game health capacity, and a heavy civic anchor that wants road access.
   *Look:* cross-shaped wards growing taller, an emergency canopy with blinking ambulances, and a helipad with a spinning helicopter rotor at Lv4+. Lv5 is a medical-tower campus with a sky bridge.

3. **Retirement Village** — S6 · 2×1→3×3 · housing for retirees: pop that needs no jobs, wants Health and parks nearby, +land value.
   *Gap:* all housing currently creates job demand. This gives a lever for jobs/housing imbalance.
   *Look:* bungalows around a bowling green and pond, growing into a garden apartment terrace with a solarium.

## B. Culture & tourism. Landmarks don't form a loop

Landmarks give flat income and land value. Nothing turns visitors into a system.
Proposal: add a **Tourism** metric. Landmarks, historical wonders, museums, parks and beaches generate visitors. Hotels capture them as income, and visitors also add traffic trips.

4. **Public Library** — S3 · 1×1→2×2 · +Education +happiness. Synergy "Reading Campus" with every school.
   *Look:* columned reading room → domed rotunda → modern glass media library with a rooftop garden.

5. **City Museum** — S9 · 2×1→3×3 · +Tourism +recreation; level unlocks exhibit wings.
   *Look:* neoclassical hall with banners; dinosaur skeleton visible through a glass atrium at Lv3; I. M. Pei-style glass pyramid at Lv5. V2 is a natural-history museum with a whale skeleton on the lawn.

6. **Theater & Opera House** — S11 · 2×2→3×3 · +recreation, +night economy (boosts bars and restaurants within 6 tiles at night).
   *Look:* marquee with chaser lights → baroque opera house → Lv5 sail-roof harbor opera hall (coastal bonus).

7. **Grand Hotel** — S8 · 1×1→2×2 (tall) · converts Tourism into income; jobs.
   *Gap:* makes landmarks and beaches economically meaningful.
   *Look:* boutique inn → art-deco tower with a rooftop pool and neon sign → Lv5 resort tower with a sky bar and blinking crown. V3 is a beach resort with cabanas when built on coast.

8. **Amusement Park** — S10 · 2×2→4×4 · huge recreation, +Tourism, noise pollution.
   *Look:* carousel (rotor) → Ferris wheel (rotor) → wooden coaster → drop tower and log flume → Lv5 castle-centered park with fireworks sparks at night.

9. **Zoo** — S9 · 2×2→4×3 · recreation +Tourism, education.
   *Look:* enclosures with animated animals, reusing the livestock critter system: giraffes, elephants, penguins on ice, a monkey island. Levels add a safari ride and aviary dome.

10. **Aquarium** — S12 · coast · 2×1→3×3 · Tourism, education.
    *Look:* wave-shaped building with a glass tunnel and dolphin-show pool; Lv5 has a whale-shark tank wall glowing blue at night.

11. **Sports Stadium** — S12 · 3×3→4×4 · recreation +Tourism. Match-day traffic spikes every in-game evening, so it rewards transit.
    *Look:* bleachers → bowl → covered arena → Lv5 retractable-roof stadium with floodlight towers (blink) and fireworks on wins.

## C. Power & infrastructure gaps

12. **Battery Storage Farm** — S9 · 1×1→3×2 · stores surplus daytime solar/wind and releases it at night.
    *Gap:* live solar drops at night with nothing to buffer it, so solar-heavy cities fail every night.
    *Look:* rows of shipping-container batteries with cooling fans (rotors) and green/amber charge-level LEDs that drain visibly at night.

13. **Telecom Mast** — S6 · 1×1 (grows tall) · Connectivity coverage (new radius service): boosts offices, data centers and banks in range; fixes "commerce works anywhere".
    *Look:* lattice mast → microwave dishes → Lv5 needle TV tower with a revolving observation deck (rotor) and blinking beacons.

14. **Lighthouse** — S6 · coast · 1×1 · cuts storm losses for boats/ships, +maritime income in radius, +Tourism.
    *Look:* striped tower with a rotating lamp (rotor and emissive beam). Levels add a keeper's cottage, foghorn and museum. V0 New England / V1 red-white Scandinavian / V2 stone Breton / V3 iron screw-pile.

15. **Seawall & Flood Barrier** — S10 · coast network piece · protects tiles behind it from storm-surge flooding (new storm event).
    *Gap:* storms only cause lightning. Coastal building has no risk and no defense.
    *Look:* stone wall → riprap with promenade → Lv5 Thames-Barrier-style rotating gates.

## D. Industry & supply chains. Production goes nowhere

Factories, farms and mines produce abstract jobs and income. There is no goods flow linking them to shops or to rail freight.
Proposal: add a light **Goods** chain. Raw producers make goods, warehouses and ports move them, and shops and malls consume them (unsupplied shops earn −30%). Freight trains and trucks carry goods visibly.

16. **Logistics Warehouse** — S6 · 2×1→4×3 · distributes Goods to shops within 10 tiles; trucks spawn on roads.
    *Look:* corrugated shed with loading docks → automated warehouse with conveyors → Lv5 robotic fulfillment center with drone pads.

17. **Lumber Mill** — S3 · 2×1→3×3 · makes Goods; +50% if trees/pine/redwood are within 4 tiles (and slowly consumes adjacent trees).
    *Look:* log pond, spinning saw blade (rotor) and sawdust puffs (smoke); stacks of planks grow per level.

18. **Stone Quarry** — S4 · 2×2→4×3 · Goods; must touch hills, mountains or canyons; dust pollution.
    *Look:* terraced cut in grey stone with derrick crane, blocks on carts and haul trucks.

19. **Fishing Wharf** — S4 · coast · 1×2→3×3 · Food from the sea (needs open water within 3 tiles); synergy with Restaurants ("Fresh Catch").
    *Look:* wooden pier with fishing boats bobbing, drying nets, a fish market with gulls; Lv5 is a cannery with a fleet.

20. **Container Port** — S13 · coast · 3×2→4×4 · exports Goods for big income; huge Rail Freight synergy.
    *Look:* quay with gantry cranes swinging containers (rotors), container stacks, a docked container ship and straddle carriers.

## E. Transit. The ladder is missing middle rungs

Today the transit ladder runs bus → heavy rail with big land cost. There is nothing for dense downtowns or mountains.

21. **Metro Station** — S14 · 1×1 · underground transit: stations within 12 tiles auto-link by tunnel, so no surface track. High trip relief.
    *Look:* glass entrance pavilion with an "M" sign → plaza with escalators → Lv5 grand interchange hall with skylights.

22. **Tram Line** — S8 · network piece placed *on* existing roads (road + rails + catenary). Medium relief without demolishing streets.
    *Look:* rails embedded in asphalt, catenary poles, a little red tram running. Levels go from heritage to modern low-floor trams.

23. **Cable Car / Gondola Station** — S10 · 1×1 pair · links two stations up to 10 tiles apart over anything (water, mountains); +Tourism near mountains and landmarks.
    *Look:* alpine station with cabins gliding along cables (moving meshes); Lv5 is a glass-cabin aerial tramway.

## F. Civic life & safety

24. **Town Square & Market Plaza** — S2 · 2×2→3×3 · +happiness, weekly market (food bonus); hosts festivals (temporary happiness and tourism events) when happiness ≥ 80.
    *Look:* cobbled square with clock tower, market stalls and a fountain; strings of lights and a festival stage at higher levels.

25. **Disaster Response Center** — S12 · 2×2→3×3 · cuts fire spread, speeds rebuilding after fires, storms and invasions, and hosts the flood-barrier and evacuation logic.
    *Look:* orange-and-white HQ with rescue trucks, a training tower and a spinning radar/weather dome; Lv5 adds helicopters and a command tower with beacons.

### How they slot into stages

| Stage | New elements |
|---|---|
| 2–4 | Clinic, Town Square, Library, Lumber Mill, Quarry, Fishing Wharf |
| 6–9 | Retirement Village, Telecom Mast, Lighthouse, Warehouse, Grand Hotel, Battery Storage, Museum, Zoo, Tram Line |
| 10–14 | Amusement Park, Seawall, Cable Car, Theater, Aquarium, Stadium, Disaster Response, Hospital (S7), Container Port, Metro |

These need three new systems: **Health**, **Tourism** and **Goods**. They are deliberately small, and they reuse the existing supply/demand meter UI.
