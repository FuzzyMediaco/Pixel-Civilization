# Matched island views

The comparison uses one 15-site starter, one 93-site district and one 156-site skyline. Each of the ten production eras and Original renders the exact same named parcels, spans, rotations, levels and variants at the same stage, clock time, viewport and camera. See the [camera and layout receipts](captures.json) and [compiled renderer identifiers](render-build.json). The [tour receipts](tours.json) cover the additional 27 era-specific playable islands. Their local gardens, market sites and skyline accents make each tour distinct.

## Independent recognition exercise

Give each of five reviewers the [street board](unlabeled-street.webp) and [skyline board](unlabeled-skyline.webp) independently. The orders differ. Ask them to name each era, then identify a home, market, utility, depot and landmark at normal gameplay zoom. Record the reviewed commit, device and responses before opening the [answer key](recognition-key.json). The design plan targets four correct reviewers out of five per view, plus four recognizable functions. Human ratings are pending.
