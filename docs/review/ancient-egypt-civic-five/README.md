[Compare all four Level 3 variants before and after](matched-civic-five.webp). The reading rooms and packet yards occupy low terraces. An axial stair leads to the judgment seat. A tower stands beside the scribes’ square and one guarded door serves the grain treasury.

The gallery used the same camera, 185-pixel cells and idle daylight for each matched pair. The [Level 5 close views](details.webp) use 300-pixel cells, a 24-degree camera angle and staffed service counters.

The [previous civic scan](before.json) flagged 144 neighboring presentations and 150 close variant pairs. The [final scan](after.json) flagged zero neighbors, close variants or flat adjacent upgrades at the standard threshold. A second scan compared different slots at each level and ten nearby catalog families. All [4,800 distances](neighbors.json) exceeded 0.12. The most detailed model uses 9,627 voxels. Every model fits its original parcel and exposes at least one lit surface.
